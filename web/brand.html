Brand kit · v2.0

A storybook spread on cream parchment.

Kivo's chrome is deliberately quiet — warm paper, hairline borders, no shadows — so a small cast of hand-drawn characters and a scatter of punctuation can carry all the personality. Colour is a marker, never decoration.

01 — The mark

Voice comes in bright. Text settles into ink.

Three waveform bars — sky, sun, ember — resolving into three lines of set text in charcoal. The colour is the point: what you say arrives loud and messy, what Kivo hands back is calm. Every element is one 3.2-unit stroke on a 32-unit grid with fully rounded caps.

Minimum sizes

Below 24 px the full mark's three text lines merge. This is a measured limit, not a preference — use the compact mark instead.

02 — The cast

Five characters and a scatter of punctuation.

The mascots do the emotional work so the interface doesn't have to. Flat fills from the accent palette, thin charcoal strokes, dot eyes and stick limbs. The confetti is punctuation, because punctuation is precisely what Kivo adds.

Punctuation confetti

Scattered asymmetrically into negative space, never overlapping type, never inside a card's text column.

03 — Colour

Warm paper, one dark action, bright markers.

Surfaces are warm off-white, never screen white. Text is warm brown, never cool grey. Ink is the only fill a button ever gets. Every other colour is a marker on text or an illustration fill.

04 — Typography

Bricolage for the voice, Inter for the work.

Display and hero headings use Bricolage Grotesque at 500 — slightly condensed, slightly playful, the thing that makes a heading feel drawn rather than set. Everything else is Inter. Never set display sizes in Inter.

05 — Surfaces and components

Pressed into paper, never floating.

The signature technique is a 1 px inset hairline in stone, not a drop shadow. Only the dark card gets real elevation, and nothing else on the page ever does.

Buttons

Two pill variants only. Ink for the single serious action, sand for its partner. Ember is a text link and never a filled button.

06 — Rules

What holds this together.

Do

  • ✓Define cards with inset 0 0 0 1px stone. Pressed into paper.
  • ✓Set every display and hero heading in Bricolage Grotesque at 500.
  • ✓Let ember carry demo links and inline accents.
  • ✓Scatter mascots asymmetrically so centred type stays the focal point.
  • ✓Use 10 px radius for cards and nav; pills only for buttons and badges.

Do not

  • ✕Use a drop shadow heavier than rgba(0,0,0,.04), except the dark card.
  • ✕Fill a button with blue, ember, or any accent. Ink is the only fill.
  • ✕Introduce a gradient. The system is strictly flat.
  • ✕Separate surfaces white-on-white — shift to stone or to ink.
  • ✕Use warm red for anything but destructive and error states.
07 — Voice

Warm on the surface, exact underneath.

The illustrations are playful. The claims are not. Kivo records, recognises and inserts — it does not listen, understand or help, and the copy never says it does. A storybook look does not license a storybook promise.

Write

  • ✓“Text appears at your cursor in under 300 ms.”
  • ✓“Kivo could not insert text into this app, so it copied it to your clipboard instead.”
  • ✓“Hold a key. Speak. It's text.”

Not

  • ✕“Lightning-fast, seamless AI dictation!”
  • ✕“Oops! Something went wrong.”
  • ✕“Kivo listens and understands what you mean.”
