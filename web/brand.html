Brand kit · v1.0

Precise, not magical.

Voice products default to purple gradients and glowing orbs — the visual language of something happening that you cannot see. Kivo is a tool that does a specific thing accurately, so it is monochrome, geometric and hairline-ruled. Emphasis comes from inversion and weight, never hue.

01 — The mark

The product, drawn literally.

Three waveform bars on the left resolving into three lines of set text on the right. Every element is one 3.2-unit stroke on a 32-unit grid with fully rounded caps — the bars and the lines are the same stroke turned ninety degrees. The wide gap in the middle is where the transformation is implied. Do not close it.

02 — Minimum sizes

Below 24 px, switch marks.

The full mark's three text lines merge into a smudge at small sizes. This is not a guideline, it is a measured limit — which is why the compact variant exists. Compare the two at 16 and 20 px.

03 — Colour

One ramp. No accent.

Kivo is monochrome, and adding a colour is a brand change rather than a design decision. A primary button is not “the blue one”, it is the one filled with ink. Ink is #0A0A0A, not pure black — black against white is harsh at paragraph length and reads as unconsidered.

04 — Typography

Inter, and mono as a texture.

Inter for the interface, JetBrains Mono for anything measured, versioned or typed by a machine. Both are open-licensed, so a fork inherits no licence trap. Tracking tightens as type grows — display set at default tracking looks loose.

05 — Space, shape, elevation

Hairlines, not shadows.

A 1 px border is how Kivo says “this is a distinct thing”. Shadows are permitted only on chrome that genuinely floats above the operating system — the dictation overlay and menus — and there are exactly two of them. Nothing else casts a shadow.

06 — Usage

Where the boundaries are.

Kivo's code is MIT licensed. The name and the marks are not — they stay with the author, so that a fork gets its own identity rather than inheriting this one.

Do

  • ✓Use the lockup at 120 px wide or more, the mark alone below that.
  • ✓Keep clear space equal to one waveform bar height on all sides.
  • ✓Use the compact mark below 24 px.
  • ✓Invert to white on ink. That is the intended dark treatment.
  • ✓Say “a fork of Kivo”, or use the logo to write about the project.

Do not

  • ✕Recolour it, add a gradient, or give it a shadow.
  • ✕Stretch it non-uniformly, rotate it, or close the central gap.
  • ✕Rebuild the wordmark in a typeface. It is drawn; no font matches it.
  • ✕Place it on a busy image, or inside another container shape.
  • ✕Use the name or mark as the identity of your own fork or product.
07 — Voice

Kivo does not listen. It records.

Never anthropomorphise the product. It records, recognises and inserts — it does not listen, understand, think or help. The credibility of everything Kivo claims about privacy rests on not pretending otherwise.

Write

  • ✓“Text appears at your cursor in under 300 ms.”
  • ✓“Kivo could not insert text into this app, so it copied it to your clipboard instead.”
  • ✓“Hold a key. Speak. It's text.”

Not

  • ✕“Lightning-fast, seamless AI dictation!”
  • ✕“Oops! Something went wrong.”
  • ✕“Kivo listens and understands what you mean.”
