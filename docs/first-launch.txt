Kivo — opening it the first time
================================

macOS will refuse to open Kivo and say:

    "Kivo" Not Opened
    Apple could not verify "Kivo" is free of malware that may harm your
    Mac or compromise your privacy.

Nothing is wrong with the download. Kivo is not signed with a paid Apple
Developer ID, and that is the message macOS shows for any such app.


ON macOS 15 (SEQUOIA) AND LATER  --  including macOS 26
-------------------------------------------------------
Control-clicking and choosing "Open" NO LONGER WORKS. Apple removed that
bypass in macOS 15. Do this instead:

  1. Drag Kivo into Applications.
  2. Double-click it once and dismiss the warning.
     This step matters -- it is what makes the button in step 4 appear.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down to Security. You will see
     "Kivo was blocked to protect your Mac".
     Click "Open Anyway", authenticate, and confirm.

You only do this once.


FASTER, IF YOU ARE COMFORTABLE IN TERMINAL
------------------------------------------
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kivo.app

That removes the download quarantine flag. Only run it on software you
trust and have verified -- see the checksum note below.


ON macOS 14 AND EARLIER
-----------------------
  Control-click Kivo in Applications > Open > Open.


VERIFY WHAT YOU DOWNLOADED
--------------------------
The SHA-256 of this disk image is published on the release page it came
from. Check it before removing any quarantine flag:

  shasum -a 256 <the .dmg you downloaded>


AFTER IT OPENS
--------------
Kivo lives in the menu bar. It has no Dock icon and no window until you
choose Settings from the menu bar icon.

It needs two permissions, and explains each before asking:

  * Microphone     -- requested on your first dictation.
  * Accessibility  -- System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
                      This is how Kivo sees your hotkey and inserts text.
                      It reads nothing from your screen.

Then hold Right Option, speak, and let go.
